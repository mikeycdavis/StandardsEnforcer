## What

A guard in this repository reports a property of a subject. This feature owns the prior question:

> **Did the guard reach its subject at all?**

A guard that could not — because a capability was absent, because an extraction matched nothing,
because the collection it iterated was empty — must say so. It must not report the subject's
property as established, and it must not be silent.

## Why

The three member stories are the same sentence found three times in eight days, in three unrelated
mechanisms:

| Story | The guard | How it reported success having examined nothing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ST-08](ST-08.md) | four symlink provenance cases | the capability was absent, so they skipped, and the run went green |
| [ST-11](ST-11.md) | the structural half of `test/adapter-policy-binding.test.mjs` | CRLF bytes, `indexOf` returned `-1`, and both assertions ran against the empty string. Measured: `body.length` **0** |
| [ST-12](ST-12.md) | every other test that derives structure from source text | unknown, deliberately — the audit ST-11 declined to perform |

ST-08 states the general case: *"A control with a correct assertion that is never evaluated is
indistinguishable from a control that holds — until something runs it."* ST-11 states the harder
half: *"a skip is at least visible in the output, whereas a vacuous assertion is indistinguishable
from a satisfied one."*

This is [EP-06](EP-06.md)'s property, one layer in. EP-06 asks whether a reported claim is true;
this feature asks whether anything was looked at before the claim was made.

## Why not FE-14, which is the same shape

[FE-14](FE-14.md) is the direct precedent for the mechanism — `ENFORCER_REQUIRE_ORACLE=1` is the
pattern all three members reach for — and ST-08 checked it first and rejected it:

> *"FE-14 is scoped by name to the oracle, and the capability here is different, as is the remedy's
> trigger. Filed as a sibling under the shared epic so neither has to be widened to accommodate the
> other."*

That reasoning is why this feature exists rather than FE-14 being broadened. FE-14 owns one
capability and closed on hosted evidence for that capability; this feature owns the general
property, and the two stay separately falsifiable.

## Acceptance

Drawn from the members' own text, not composed:

1. **A guard that could not reach its subject does not report that subject's property as
   established** — it reports that it could not. Absence of a subject is a finding, never a default.
2. **The extraction pattern is used wherever a guard derives its subject from text**, in this order,
   each step itself asserted (ST-11): normalise → locate the boundary → **assert the boundary was
   found** → assert the region is the intended seam → strip comments, asserting the strip left code
   and removed prose → only then, the structural assertions.
3. **A capability an environment claims is asserted, not assumed.** Where a run declares a capability
   as part of the authority it asserts, inability to exercise it is a failure and not a skip (ST-08).
   This is **not a generic zero-skip rule**: platform-conditioned skips are legitimate and stay.
4. **Each mechanism is shown firing on a planted defect and silent on the remedy**, against a real
   run (ST-11's falsification in both directions; ST-08's both-arms requirement, where each arm is
   reachable from every platform via a stubbed probe).
5. **A count is never the mechanism.** Asserting the number of tests, skips or matches is fully
   satisfiable while the defect stands — ST-08 (*"no shell reads a count"*), ST-10 and ST-13 each
   ruled it out independently.
6. **No guard is repaired by rewording the source it scans** (ST-11, ST-12). The subject of a guard
   is a mechanism, not a spelling.

## Status

`IN_PROGRESS`, and it is [ST-12](ST-12.md) that holds it open: the audit that would establish
whether ST-11 was one specimen or a class has not been run. ST-08 and ST-11 are COMPLETE with their
evidence recorded on their own items.

**Not closable on the two closed members.** Closing this while ST-12 is unstarted would assert the
class is bounded on the evidence of two instances of it — which is the shape of the defect the
feature is named for.

## What this feature does not own

Whether any guard's **assertions are correct**. ST-08 draws the line: *"Nothing here establishes
that the link-containment assertions are correct — that is ADR 0005's, already tested — only that
they run wherever the pipeline says they did."* That boundary is this feature's throughout.

It also does not own the **composition** of the authoritative suite or the ability of the pipeline
to run at all. Those are [EP-07](EP-07.md)'s.
