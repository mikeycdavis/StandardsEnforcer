## What

Given a repository and an immutable *(standards repository, release tag, commit SHA)* identity,
execute the official standards implementation and report its result without independently recreating
or reinterpreting the standards.

## Why

The first thing an enforcer must prove is that it is a transport and not a second opinion.

## Notes

Identity is a triple and all three must resolve consistently before anything runs: the tag resolves
to exactly that commit, a tree is materialised at that commit cached by SHA, and the materialised
checkout's HEAD is that commit.

The third check is not redundant with the first. One asks what the tag points at; the other
establishes what is about to run.

Verified as an oracle — MachineLearningStandards `v1.4.0` against itself and against Numerai, with a
payload-fidelity comparison rather than an assertion that the transport was faithful.
