## What

`authorisedReviewers` is a configured list of names. Real human-attestation identity — review
provenance, CODEOWNERS, approvals — is a different claim.

## Why

Scope dispositions count only from a named authorised reviewer. The strength of that guarantee is
currently the strength of a list somebody wrote down.
