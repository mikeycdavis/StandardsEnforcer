## What

[FE-22](FE-22.md) owns the property that a guard must establish it examined its subject. This
feature owns making that property **executable**, so that it is enforced rather than remembered. It
is the mechanism half of the same concern, kept as a sibling so that the property and the mechanism
stay separately falsifiable.

The three members state one property at three widths, in their own words:

> **ST-15.** Any assurance test that derives a verdict from a collection must prove at execution
> time that the collection is non-empty before a success verdict can be accepted.
>
> **ST-16.** Any assurance verdict derived from an empty subject must fail, whatever shape derived
> it — independent of whether an accumulator was built.
>
> **ST-17.** A stage in `ci/checks.sh` that did not examine the subject it names must not be able to
> report that stage's property as established.

## Why

Each member exists because the one before it left the same hole reachable:

| Story | The gap it found | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ST-15](ST-15.md) | The word `liveness` appeared in three places on `main`: a comment and two callers. *"A convention documented in prose, honoured by two files, enforced by nothing."* A probe whose subject was empty passed inside the authoritative surface | `COMPLETE` |
| [ST-16](ST-16.md) | ST-15's discriminator recognised one shape, an accumulator over an iteration. Three further probes with empty subjects and no accumulator passed unobjected | `COMPLETE` |
| [ST-17](ST-17.md) | The same defect one layer out: a `ci/checks.sh` stage outside the enumerated test surface reported success over an empty subject, and the authoritative route certified a false claim | `NOT_STARTED` |

ST-15 and ST-16 made liveness executable over the enumerated test surface. ST-17 found that the
authoritative CI route contains assurance surfaces of equal standing that sit outside that surface.

## Acceptance

Drawn from the members' shared shape rather than composed. Every member is red-first, and all three
reject the same shortcuts:

1. **The falsifier exists first and is observed passing** against the base the work starts from. A
   falsifier never observed passing has not established the defect (ST-15, ST-16, ST-17).
2. **A standing mechanism reddens it**, over the authoritative surface, independent of the shape
   that derived the verdict (ST-15, ST-16) and for a CI stage as well as a test (ST-17).
3. **The honest vacuous case stays green and says so distinguishably.** A repository that truly has
   an empty subject must still pass; ST-17 names `NOT_EXERCISED` as the existing vocabulary for it.
4. **The mechanism proves its own liveness**, per [ST-11](ST-11.md): shown firing on a planted
   violation and silent on the remedy, against a real file or run rather than a string.
5. **A count, a coverage threshold, a naming convention or a prose rule is never the mechanism.**
   Each is fully satisfiable while the defect stands. ST-15 and ST-16 rule these out explicitly, and
   ST-17 rules out asserting a dependency count for the same reason.

## Status

`IN_PROGRESS`: two of three members are `COMPLETE` and [ST-17](ST-17.md) is `NOT_STARTED`. ST-17 is
also where the general case is left open: `ci/checks.sh` has five stages, ST-17 establishes the gap
for one, and whether the others share it is recorded there as unmeasured.

**Closure is not inferred from the members' status.** Whether the feature closes when ST-17 does, or
whether the unmeasured stages need a member of their own first, is for the owner to decide.

## What this feature does not own

Whether any guard's **assertions are correct**, only that they ran on something. That boundary is
[FE-22](FE-22.md)'s and is kept here.

It also does not own which bytes an oracle-dependent test consumed ([FE-26](FE-26.md)) or which
tests the runner discovers ([ST-13](ST-13.md), under [FE-25](FE-25.md)).
