## What

Whether one standard's result should be a precondition for another's — the shape StandardsOrchestrator
owns as `prediction → betting`.

## Why

The orchestrator's argument is that a dependency between two domains belongs to composition rather
than to either pack, since neither can see the other. That argument was found convincing and would be
expensive to re-derive.

## Notes

The one item in this backlog carrying an explicit milestone marker from repository evidence: the
orchestrator reconciliation records it as **"Revisit at M5+"**, under NOT YET EARNED.

It is not earned today. The enforcer has no cross-pack dependency concept and no case requiring one.
The standing rule holds — a concept is added when something forces it, not when the argument for it
reads well.
