## What

`artifacts/backlog/README.md` is the surface [IN-01](IN-01.md) treats as this project's
authoritative liveness record. This feature owns the property that its mechanical claims — counts,
percentages, progress tuples, the in-flight list and the tree — can be reproduced or checked from
`items/*.md` by something runnable, and that the page's own description of itself is true.

## Why

The tracker opened by declaring itself generated:

```text
<!-- GENERATED FILE - do not edit by hand. Re-run the backlog script after changing anything in items/. -->
```

and no such script existed. The derivation was a human procedure wearing a machine's label.

**Recorded on a specimen rather than a worry.** Adding [ST-08](ST-08.md) and [ST-09](ST-09.md) in
`c45a4e4` required hand-editing **five** separate derived figures, *"plus the tree. Each was an
opportunity to be quietly wrong, and the only check available was arithmetic performed by the same
party that wrote the numbers"* (ST-10).

**Why this sits under [EP-07](EP-07.md).** ST-10 reads its own subject as EP-06's — *"This is
EP-06's property applied to the backlog itself"* — and as a description of the **defect** that
reading is sound and is not withdrawn: a surface reporting figures as though computed, with nothing
establishing that they were, is what EP-06 was created for. What places this feature under EP-07 is
the **remedy**, and the owner's instruction that the backlog's tooling belongs under the epic
responsible for repository workflow tooling. EP-07's `What` is that machinery — *"the entry points
that stage a commit and start the authoritative pipeline, the composition of the suite that pipeline
runs, and this repository's own tooling for the records it derives"* — and `scripts/backlog.mjs`
with `test/backlog-tracker.test.mjs` is exactly that. EP-06's `What` is narrower than the first
reading suggests: *"StandardsEnforcer's own authoritative assurance surfaces must establish the
claims they report"*, owned by an epic created for a green suite that exercised nothing, whose scope
note warns it *"must not become a place to file findings merely because a test was involved"*.
EP-07 admitted this feature by widening its scope boundary in the same change, which it says plainly
there; both readings stay on the record rather than one being erased.

It is kept out of [FE-22](FE-22.md) because FE-22's subject is a guard over code reaching its
subject; this one's subject is a derived record agreeing with its source.

## Acceptance

Criteria 1 and 4 are stated as ST-10 stated them, narrowly and on purpose. Criteria 2 and 3 are
**composed**, and are marked where they depart from ST-10's stated acceptance property — a criterion
back-derived from a closure narrative and then declared met by that same narrative establishes
nothing, so its provenance is named rather than presented as quotation.

1. **Given the item files, there is one deterministic way to reproduce or check the tracker, such
   that a stale hand-edited count cannot remain green.** A checker that recomputes and fails on
   disagreement satisfies this as well as a generator does, and is the smaller change.
2. **The header ends up telling the truth**: either the file is produced by something runnable, or
   it stops claiming to be. That much is ST-10's, stated in its Notes before the work. The tail —
   **and a test asserts which** — is **derived**, from ST-10's *closure narrative* (*"the header no
   longer claims to be generated, and a test asserts that it does not"*), which records what was
   built rather than what was required in advance.
3. **Derived, not stated.** The check runs where the repository's verdict is carried, not as an
   optional local step. ST-10 states no such acceptance property; this is back-derived from its
   *closure narrative* — *"`test/backlog-tracker.test.mjs` runs it under `npm test`, which is what
   `ci/checks.sh` executes on both the container and the hosted runner"* — which describes what was
   done, and so cannot also be the evidence that it was required. Kept because it is substantively
   right: a check that only a local step runs cannot fail a verdict. Relabelled, not deleted.
4. **The check's own liveness is established by mutation**, not by agreement with today's numbers.
   *"A reconciliation script that agrees with today's numbers is not evidence that a wrong number
   would be caught"* (ST-10) — which is [FE-22](FE-22.md)'s property, applied here by the same
   argument rather than by shared parentage.

## Closed 2026-09-18 — on ST-10's evidence, not on new work

All four criteria were met on 2026-08-16 by [ST-10](ST-10.md), with the caveat the Acceptance
section now carries: criterion 3 and the tail of criterion 2 were derived from this same narrative,
so it confirms that they hold and not that they were ever required. `scripts/backlog.mjs` recomputes
every mechanical claim and compares; `test/backlog-tracker.test.mjs` runs it under `npm test`, which
`ci/checks.sh` executes on both the container and the hosted runner; the header no longer claims to
be generated and a test asserts that it does not; and the tree-and-list comparison was neutered as a
mutation, leaving the primary check green while seven mutations went red.

The closing date is the date this feature was written, not the date the property was established —
2026-08-16 is that date, and it is recorded on ST-10 with its evidence. Backdating the feature to
sit before its own creation would be a nicer-looking record and a false one.
