## What

`artifacts/backlog/README.md` is the surface [IN-01](IN-01.md) treats as this project's
authoritative liveness record. This feature owns the property that its mechanical claims — counts,
percentages, progress tuples, the in-flight list and the tree — can be reproduced or checked from
`items/*.md` by something runnable, and that the page's own description of itself is true.

## Why

The tracker opened by declaring itself generated:

```text
<!-- GENERATED FILE - do not edit by hand. Re-run the backlog script after changing anything in items/. -->
```

and no such script existed. The derivation was a human procedure wearing a machine's label.

**Recorded on a specimen rather than a worry.** Adding [ST-08](ST-08.md) and [ST-09](ST-09.md) in
`c45a4e4` required hand-editing **five** separate derived figures, *"plus the tree. Each was an
opportunity to be quietly wrong, and the only check available was arithmetic performed by the same
party that wrote the numbers"* (ST-10).

**Why this is EP-06's and not EP-07's.** The subject is a document this repository publishes about
itself, not the machinery that builds or runs it. ST-10 states the parentage in its own words —
*"This is EP-06's property applied to the backlog itself"* — and it is the same property EP-06 was
created for: a surface reporting figures as though computed, with nothing establishing that they
were. It is kept out of [FE-22](FE-22.md) because FE-22's subject is a guard over code reaching its
subject; this one's subject is a derived record agreeing with its source.

## Acceptance

Stated as ST-10 stated it, narrowly and on purpose:

1. **Given the item files, there is one deterministic way to reproduce or check the tracker, such
   that a stale hand-edited count cannot remain green.** A checker that recomputes and fails on
   disagreement satisfies this as well as a generator does, and is the smaller change.
2. **The header ends up telling the truth**: either the file is produced by something runnable, or
   it stops claiming to be — and a test asserts which.
3. **The check runs where the repository's verdict is carried**, not as an optional local step.
4. **The check's own liveness is established by mutation**, not by agreement with today's numbers.
   *"A reconciliation script that agrees with today's numbers is not evidence that a wrong number
   would be caught"* (ST-10) — which is [FE-22](FE-22.md)'s property, applied here by the same
   argument rather than by shared parentage.

## Closed 2026-09-18 — on ST-10's evidence, not on new work

All four criteria were met on 2026-08-16 by [ST-10](ST-10.md). `scripts/backlog.mjs` recomputes
every mechanical claim and compares; `test/backlog-tracker.test.mjs` runs it under `npm test`, which
`ci/checks.sh` executes on both the container and the hosted runner; the header no longer claims to
be generated and a test asserts that it does not; and the tree-and-list comparison was neutered as a
mutation, leaving the primary check green while seven mutations went red.

The closing date is the date this feature was written, not the date the property was established —
2026-08-16 is that date, and it is recorded on ST-10 with its evidence. Backdating the feature to
sit before its own creation would be a nicer-looking record and a false one.

## Bounded

**Prose on the page is not checked and is not claimed to be.** Nine figures are compared. Nothing
here validates that the *hierarchy* is well-shaped beyond parent existence, reachability, prefix
agreement and the closed-date pairing — the level-skip rule this repository's own checker
deliberately omits was, until the 2026-09-18 reparenting, unvalidated, and was enforced from outside
rather than by `scripts/backlog.mjs`.
