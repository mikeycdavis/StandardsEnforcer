## What

Independent standards repositories exist and are authoritative for their own domains. This theme
covers making compliance with a *released* standard a condition of merging, without any enforcement
layer acquiring domain knowledge of its own.

## Why

Guidance in a `CLAUDE.md` saying "follow the standards" is not enforcement. A standards pack that
nobody is required to run governs nothing.

## Notes

The theme outlives any particular enforcer. It is the enduring area of value, not the current
implementation of it.
