## What

The remedy for ST-06 is GitHub's `workflows` ruleset rule, which pins a repository, a path and a
commit — none of them under the governed pull request's control. `assessGate` already requires it and
returns `GATE_CONFIG_INVALID` without one. Nothing has confirmed it behaves as modelled on GitHub.

## Why

M4's asymmetry, stated in its own evidence: **the hole is proven, the patch is not.** A remedy
asserted only against an injected platform is exactly the position M2 was in before M4 ran, and M4 is
the reason to distrust it.

## Notes

**Blocked on an organisation.** Required workflows are an organisation feature; the rule was rejected
on a personal-account repository:

```json
{"message":"Validation Failed",
 "errors":["Invalid rule 'workflows': Invalid parameter workflows: Workflow error at index 0: "],
 "status":"422"}
```

That 422 is measured, not assumed, and it is the whole dependency: this needs an organisation to
exist before the experiment can be constructed.

**Dormant, not failed.** The status token is `BLOCKED` because that is the vocabulary's word for
"cannot proceed because of a dependency", but the epistemic character matters and the two must not
read the same: this experiment has never run. It has not produced a negative result, and nothing here
suggests the required-workflows remedy does not work. What is established is an *accessibility
boundary* — the environment to test it is unavailable. Do not record this as evidence against the
remedy, do not open a replacement enforcement-root investigation because this path is quiet, and do
not substitute a different experiment under this milestone.

Distinct from ST-06's stated limitation, and both matter. ST-06 could not establish separation from
an administrator; this one cannot yet establish the remedy at all.
