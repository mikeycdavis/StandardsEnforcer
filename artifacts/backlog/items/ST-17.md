## What

[ST-15](ST-15.md) and [ST-16](ST-16.md) made guard liveness executable over the **enumerated test
surface** — `scripts/test-surface.mjs`, whose `TEST_DIR` is `test`. The authoritative check list in
`ci/checks.sh` contains assurance surfaces of equal standing that are outside that surface, and one
of them can report success having examined nothing.

> **Acceptance property.** A stage in `ci/checks.sh` that did not examine the subject it names must
> not be able to report that stage's property as established.

## Why

Demonstrated 2026-09-06 against the landed revision, by running the repository's own Docker route
rather than by reading. This is the parent-level falsification attempt [ST-16](ST-16.md)'s Notes
require of [EP-06](EP-06.md), and it found a shape.

**`npm ci` does not catch the defect.** Measured first, because the claim depends on it: with
`package.json` declaring `"acorn": "^8.18.0"` against a lockfile pinning `8.18.0`, `npm ci` exits
**0**. `ci/dependency-posture.mjs` rule 2 is the sole guard for exact pinning.

Two arms, both `./scripts/ci.sh --oracle=<oracle>`, the exact-commit route (`git archive`):

| Arm | Commit | Defect | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A — control | `3e10a09` | `acorn` unpinned to `^8.18.0` | **FAIL** at `pinned-install-invariant`, naming both findings. `Checks executed: environment` |
| B — the probe | `d0b92b8` | A, **plus** the check's subject emptied | **PASS**. `Checks executed: environment pinned-install-invariant oracle-readiness credential-hygiene test-suite` |

Arm B's second defect is one line, and deliberately a plausible rename rather than sabotage:

```js
-const declared = { ...(pkg.dependencies ?? {}), ...(pkg.devDependencies ?? {}) };
+const declared = { ...(pkg.deps ?? {}), ...(pkg.devDeps ?? {}) };
```

That empties `names`, so the check takes **its own already-shipped early-exit path** —

```
package.json declares no dependencies — the pinned-install invariant is vacuously true
   ok  pinned-install-invariant
```

— and exits 0. Arm A proves the underlying defect was real and detectable; Arm B proves the
authoritative route cannot tell an effective posture check from an ineffective one. **The run
certified a false claim**, not merely an unexercised one: acorn was genuinely unpinned in the commit
that passed.

The full enumerated surface ran in Arm B — 407 tests, 403 pass, 0 fail, 4 skipped — and **nothing
objected**. Tests 162–167 of that same run are the credential-hygiene stage's own guards asserting
*"required, with nothing to inspect, is FAILED and not a pass"*. The repository holds that property
for one CI stage and not for its neighbour.

## Why this is not "a module without a unit test"

A missing test is a gap in coverage; this is a reachable false green, and the two were kept apart
deliberately. `ci/credential-hygiene.mjs` and `ci/verify.mjs` are exercised by
`test/credential-hygiene.test.mjs`, `test/local-ci-verify.test.mjs` and
`test/cached-checkout-identity.test.mjs`. `ci/dependency-posture.mjs` is exercised by nothing — but
what makes it an EP-06 finding is the measured Arm B run, not the absent test.

The vacuous-success path is **not a bug introduced by the probe**. It ships at `c04653a`, is
correct on its own terms — a repository with no dependencies really does satisfy the invariant
vacuously — and is reached whenever the subject is empty *for any reason*: a rename, a
restructure, a move to workspaces. The defect is that nothing distinguishes "no dependencies" from
"could not find the dependencies".

## Acceptance

Red-first, in ST-15's order, and against the Docker route rather than by reading.

1. **Reproduce Arm B** at the base this work starts from and observe it PASS. A falsifier never
   observed passing has not established the defect.
2. **A mechanism that reddens it.** The subject must be established rather than assumed: an empty
   dependency set is a claim requiring evidence — that `package.json` genuinely declares none —
   not a default. The shape is the one `ci/credential-hygiene.mjs` already uses: name the subject
   unconditionally, and let an environment-supplied requirement make absence a failure.
3. **The honest vacuous case stays green.** A repository that truly declares no dependencies must
   still pass, and must say so distinguishably. `NOT_EXERCISED` already exists in this repository's
   vocabulary for exactly this and should be preferred over inventing another.
4. **The mechanism proves its own liveness**, per [ST-11](ST-11.md): shown firing on the planted
   defect and silent on the remedy, verified against a real run.
5. **Consider the general case.** `ci/checks.sh` has five stages; this story establishes the gap for
   one. Whether the other stages share it is unmeasured — see below.

## Explicitly not a solution

- **Adding a unit test for `ci/dependency-posture.mjs` alone.** It would raise coverage without
  touching the property: a test suite is not what certified Arm B, `ci/checks.sh` is.
- **Deleting the vacuous-success path.** It is correct for the case it names. The fix is to
  establish the subject, not to remove the branch that reports it.
- **Asserting a dependency count.** Ruled out for the reason ST-15 and ST-16 both ruled it out:
  fully satisfiable while the defect stands.

## Notes

**Scope.** EP-06's boundary — this repository's assurance about itself. Not a guarantee offered to
governed repositories.

**Unmeasured boundary, stated rather than implied.** Arm B establishes the shape for
`pinned-install-invariant`. `oracle-readiness`, `credential-hygiene` and `test-suite` were **not**
probed in this experiment; `credential-hygiene` demonstrably holds the property already, and the
other two are unknown rather than sound.

**This does not reopen [ST-16](ST-16.md).** ST-16's close condition was a clean adversarial round
against its own mechanism over the test surface, and round twenty-four met it. This is a different
surface, found by the separate parent-level evaluation ST-16's Notes require.

**[EP-06](EP-06.md) stays open**, and this is why. Its two recorded conditions are now split: the
FE-14 execution condition is met; the parent-level falsification condition is not, because the
attempt that was owed found this.
