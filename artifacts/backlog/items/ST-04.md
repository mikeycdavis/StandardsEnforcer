## What

Execute the adapter schema rather than paraphrase it, plus the three properties JSON Schema cannot
express: `passing ⊆ statuses`, entrypoint containment, and known placeholders only.

## Why

The boundary review found that the schema existed, the declarations existed, and nothing anywhere
validated one against the other — so `additionalProperties: false`, the `schemaVersion` `const`, the
entrypoint pattern and `contains` were all decorative.

## Notes

**It caught things on its first run.** The schema disagreed with three of the four released
contracts: `evaluation.arguments` required an argument *exactly* equal to `{target}`, which only
Betting's positional form satisfies. Both legitimate forms are now pinned.

The validator throws on any keyword it cannot enforce. A validator that silently skips an
unimplemented constraint reports conformance for a document it never fully checked — which is the
same false-green shape in the mechanism meant to prevent false greens.

It runs in two places on purpose: pack CI is producer assurance, enforcer load is consumer defence. A
pack having validated its declaration last Tuesday is not evidence about the bytes the enforcer just
read out of a checkout.
