## What

Every repository in the governed population has an explicit scope disposition; the absence or
staleness of that disposition is visible. Detection may require review, but it can never make the
disposition.

## Why

If a detector can decide a repository is ungoverned, then not tripping the detector is a way out of
governance — and the repository being governed is exactly the party that controls what a detector
sees.

## Notes

The asymmetry is the negative-evidence lesson from MachineLearningStandards 1.2, applied one level
out: evidence found can contradict a recorded decision; evidence not found can never confirm one.

`OUT_OF_SCOPE` is the only non-verdict a merge may proceed on, and it is bounded rather than trusted
— producible only with a named authorised reviewer, a date and a reason, demoted to
`SCOPE_REVIEW_REQUIRED` without them.
