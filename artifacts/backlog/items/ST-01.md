## What

Read every released standards pack and record its real interface — binary, verdict command, argv
shape, output envelope, exit codes, verdict vocabulary — rather than assuming a family resemblance.

## Why

Any adapter field inferred from a sibling pack is a guess that will read as a fact later.

## Notes

The census found no common abstraction where one was most tempting. Native exit codes in particular
are not a shared vocabulary: Betting exits 2 on `NOT_EVALUATED`, MachineLearning 3 on blocked, Health
4, and Financial folds blocked into 1.

That finding is what later made `exitCodes` the wrong thing to add to the adapter contract — it would
have created a second semantic projection owned by every pack, to rescue a surface derived from the
original mistake.
