## What

Given an immutable *(standards repository, release tag, commit SHA)* identity, obtain that release's
own declared invocation contract, run exactly the evaluator it names, and carry its result back
without reinterpreting what the result means.

## Why

This is the load-bearing half of ADR 0001. If the enforcer knows what a pack's verdict means, it is
an authority on that pack's domain whether or not it contains any rules.

## Notes

Closed as **`IMPLEMENTED — PORTFOLIO_BLOCKED`**. Those are two claims and the milestone deliberately
records both: the architecture is accepted and demonstrated across three heterogeneous authorities
(Betting `v1.0.1`, Mathematics `v1.0.1`, MachineLearning `v1.5.0`), while universal portfolio
acceptance stays blocked on five upstream authorities.

**Two things are deliberately not backlog items**, per the acceptance record:

- The five blocked authorities (Innovation, Engineering, Health, Financial, Prediction) each fail for
  their own named reason and have owners who are not this repository. The reopening condition is
  mechanical — the next unmet prerequisite becomes the reported reason on the next run — so it does
  not depend on anyone remembering a ticket.
- ADR 0005 case 7b, symlink escape, is SKIPPED rather than passed: this platform returns `EPERM` on
  symlink creation. It needs a capable machine, not a decision. A skip on a capable environment is
  specified as a test failure, so the gap cannot decay into folklore.

The suite stands at 163 tests, 162 pass, 1 skipped. That qualification travels with the claim and
must not be compressed into "all provenance tests pass".

## Reopened 2026-08-11

**Closed 2026-08-10. Reopened because new evidence falsified this parent's broader acceptance claim
about durable pinned identity — not because the work beneath it was unfinished.**

Stated precisely, because the two readings are easy to confuse:

```text
children:             COMPLETE — unchanged, and not reopened
historical evidence:  preserved — still true about what was established on 2026-08-10
parent EP-01:         reopened — a newly discovered false-green path in its broader guarantee
FE-13:                owns that newly discovered gap
```

The work was completed against the evidence available then. What is no longer established is the
*parent* guarantee — that identity stays pinned across runs — which is a broader claim than any child
made.

[FE-13](FE-13.md) established that `materialise()` returns on the presence of a `.enforcer-complete`
marker without re-verifying `HEAD`, so on every run after the first, the pinned identity this epic
claims to deliver is asserted by a marker file in a world-writable temporary directory rather than
established. That is a false-green path in exactly the guarantee named in this epic's title.

Three things are worth keeping straight, because they are easy to collapse:

- **The evidence cited above is still true.** `2026-08-10-m2-acceptance.md` and `11baf1d` record what
  was established on that date, and nothing here falsifies them. The historical record is not
  rewritten, and none of the six children is reopened or downgraded.
- **Every enumerated child is still COMPLETE, and correctly so.** That is the point. All listed
  children being complete did not prove the parent concept was, and the tracker would have gone on
  reporting 6/6 indefinitely.
- **`IMPLEMENTED — PORTFOLIO_BLOCKED` was, and remains, a truthful milestone state.** Reopening this
  epic is not a retraction of that; the architecture is still demonstrated across three authorities.
  The hole is in identity re-verification, which is a different claim from portfolio reach.
