## What

StandardsEnforcer's own authoritative assurance surfaces must establish the claims they report. A
green suite, a green CI run, or a quoted test count is a claim about what was exercised, and this
epic owns whether that claim is true.

## Why

This repository's entire purpose is refusing results that report more than they establish. It has no
standing to do that while its own evidence surfaces can report success without exercising the thing
they certify — and [FE-14](FE-14.md) established that they can: every oracle-dependent test skips on
a runner where MachineLearningStandards is absent, and `npm test` still exits 0.

The failure is the same shape the enforcer exists to detect, sitting inside the mechanism that
detects it. That is why it needs an owner rather than being filed wherever the affected tests happen
to live.

The two stay separate so both remain falsifiable |

[IN-01](IN-01.md) already anticipates this strand: it names *"the enforcer's own release honesty"*
beside the three independently forgeable questions. This epic is that strand, widened from release
identity to evidence.

**FE-14's own text agrees**, and was the deciding input: *"This reaches past EP-01 — `gate.test.mjs`
gates EP-02's evidence and `scope.test.mjs` gates EP-03's."* A property that spans three epics belongs
to none of them.

## Notes

**Scope boundary.** This epic owns the truthfulness of assurance *this repository produces about
itself*. It does not own the enforcement guarantees offered to governed repositories — those stay with
EP-01, EP-02 and EP-03 — and it must not become a place to file findings merely because a test was
involved.

**Not closable by a green run.** Per FE-14's closing condition, evidence that oracle-dependent tests
**executed** is required; a green run in which they skipped is the defect itself, and would close work
on the evidence of the thing it exists to prevent.

**Nothing here bears on ST-07.** There is still no live-validated authoritative GitHub Actions
enforcement root, and no amount of self-assurance work creates one.
