## What

Establish whether the adapter contract can express the interfaces Phase 0 found, and separately
whether each pack has a release that can carry one.

## Why

Two different failures look identical from a distance: a contract that cannot describe a pack, and a
pack with no truthful release to describe. Only the first is this repository's to fix.

## Notes

The contract was adequate. The packs were the constraint — which is the finding that eventually
became M2's `PORTFOLIO_BLOCKED` half.

Recorded as *four of eight* rather than as a single unsupported bucket, because after an upstream
repair the next unmet prerequisite has to become visible rather than the original label persisting.
